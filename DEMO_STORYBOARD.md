Zikim — 25s Client Demo Storyboard
=================================

Format: each frame lists visual, on-screen text, and narration (25s total).

Frame 1 — Title (0–3s)
- Visual: Centered app logo on plain background.
- On-screen text: "Zikim — Live Group Trivia"
- Narration: "Zikim — live group trivia."

Frame 2 — Hook (3–7s)
- Visual: Three large icons in a row: Create, Play, Leaderboard.
- On-screen text: "Instant rooms • Live play • Leaderboards"
- Narration: "Instant rooms, live play, real-time leaderboards."

Frame 3 — Create & Join (7–14s)
- Visual: Screen capture sequence (left-to-right):
  1) User types room name and clicks Create
  2) Click Copy Link
  3) Second browser tab opens link and enters nickname
- On-screen text: "Create • Share • Join (no app)"
- Narration: "Create a room, share the link, players join — no install."

Frame 4 — Live Play (14–20s)
- Visual: Question card appears; two players click answers; scoreboard updates (highlight changed score)
- On-screen text: "Real-time answers & scoring"
- Narration: "Questions run live and scores update instantly."

Frame 5 — Results (20–23s)
- Visual: Final leaderboard with confetti animation
- On-screen text: "Session summary"
- Narration: "Fast results and a shareable summary."

Frame 6 — Orientation map & articles (overlay or quick cut-in)
- Visual: Quick 2s overlay showing the `Orientation` map page (map thumbnail) and tiny article thumbnails for `Early History` and `Otef Aza`.
- On-screen text: "Contextual maps & articles"
- Narration (short overlay during Create & Join or Live Play): "Includes contextual maps and short articles to enrich questions."

Frame 7 — CTA (23–25s)
- Visual: Contact card with email and "Request a pilot" button
- On-screen text: "Request a pilot: your@email.com"
- Narration: "Want a pilot? Contact us."

Notes for recording
- Use 16:9, 1080p export.
- Hide any code, consoles, or secrets. Blur if needed.
- Keep cursor visible and pause 0.5–1s on clicks.
- Use captions matching the narration for accessibility.

Shot checklist (copy to follow while recording)
1. Title card (3s)
2. Hook icons (4s)
3. Create & share flow (7s)
4. Live question + answers (6s)
5. Results & confetti (3s)
6. Orientation map + article thumbnails (2–3s overlay)
7. CTA card (2s)
